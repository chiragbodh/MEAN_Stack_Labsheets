# Screenshot checklist

The assignment requires source-code and running-output screenshots for all
50 questions. These are **not yet captured on your computer**. Build logs are
verification records and do not replace the assignment's screenshots.

Take real screenshots after each practical runs. Include browser DevTools,
installation, Compass or Postman evidence where that question requests it.
Store them in the question's `screenshots/` folder inside its unit.
Mark a row Done only after checking the images show the required behavior.
